Elegant Themes published a roundup of the [7 best WordPress themes for bloggers in 2026](https://www.elegantthemes.com/blog/wordpress/best-wordpress-themes-for-bloggers). Divi is one of them for good reason — complete visual control over post layouts, archive pages, and category templates without code.

The theme you pick affects everything from page speed to how long readers stay. Here is what the comparison found.

## Key Takeaways

- **Divi wins on visual control** — custom blog templates without coding
- **GeneratePress and Kadence are faster** — lighter for pure performance
- **Astra has the most starter templates** for blogging
- **Divi's A/B testing is unique** — no other theme offers built-in split testing
- **Theme Builder + Loop Builder** let you design every aspect of your blog

## Practical Application

For content-heavy sites, Divi's Theme Builder means designing a post template once and applying it to every article. The Loop Builder adds dynamic related posts, custom grids, and category-specific layouts.

If your blog is your business, Divi's A/B testing alone justifies the cost. Test headlines, layouts, and CTAs directly inside the builder.
