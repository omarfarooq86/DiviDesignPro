"How much should I expect to pay?" It's the most common question I get from business owners looking to hire someone for their Divi site. The honest answer: **it varies**, but here's a realistic breakdown.

## Hourly Rates by Experience Level

| Level | Rate | Typical Work |
|---|---|---|
| Entry-level | $15–30/hr | Basic edits, content updates, small tasks |
| Experienced | $30–60/hr | Full website builds, redesigns, customizations |
| Senior specialist | $60–100+/hr | Complex custom work, migrations, consulting |

## Project-Based Pricing

For well-defined projects, many freelancers offer fixed prices:

| Project Type | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Landing page | $500–1,500 |
| 5-page business site | $1,500–4,000 |
| Full custom website | $3,000–8,000+ |
| WooCommerce + Divi | $4,000–12,000+ |

## What Affects the Cost?

### Complexity
A simple brochure site costs far less than a membership site with custom functionality. Every custom feature, integration, or dynamic element adds development time.

### Number of Pages
More pages means more design and testing. But page templates reduce per-page costs — it's not strictly linear.

### Design Source
Starting from a finished Figma or Sketch file is faster than designing from scratch. AI-generated mockups fall somewhere in between — they provide direction but still need interpretation.

### Content Readiness
If you provide all text and images, the project moves faster. If the freelancer needs to source or create content, expect additional cost.

### Revisions
Most freelancers include **2–3 revision rounds** in their pricing. Additional rounds are typically billed hourly.

## How to Get the Best Value

1. **Have clear requirements** — Specifics get you accurate quotes
2. **Prepare content in advance** — Saves significant time and cost
3. **Bundle work** — Multiple pages in one project cost less than separate engagements
4. **Invest in quality upfront** — The cheapest option usually costs more long-term through revisions, fixes, and lost performance

## The Bottom Line

The right Divi freelancer is an **investment in your business's online presence**. A well-built Divi site pays for itself through better conversions, improved SEO, and a professional image that builds trust. Get a detailed quote based on your specific requirements — not a generic number.
