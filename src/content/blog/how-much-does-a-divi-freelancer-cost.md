One of the most common questions I get from business owners looking to hire someone to build or fix their WordPress site is: how much should I expect to pay? It is a fair question, and the honest answer is that Divi freelancer pricing varies quite a bit.

## Pricing Models

### Hourly Rates

Most Divi freelancers charge hourly. Typical rates:
- **$15-30/hr** – Entry-level, basic edits, content updates
- **$30-60/hr** – Experienced Divi developers, full site builds
- **$60-100+/hr** – Senior specialists, complex custom work, consulting

### Project-Based Pricing

For well-defined projects, many freelancers offer fixed prices. Typical ranges:
- **Landing page** – $500-1,500
- **5-page business site** – $1,500-4,000
- **Full custom website** – $3,000-8,000+
- **eCommerce (WooCommerce + Divi)** – $4,000-12,000+

## Factors That Affect Cost

### Complexity

A simple brochure site costs far less than a membership site with custom functionality. Every custom feature, integration, or dynamic element adds development time.

### Number of Pages

More pages means more design, more content layout, and more testing time. But it's not strictly linear — page templates can reduce per-page costs.

### Design Requirements

Starting from a design file (Figma, Sketch, or AI-generated mockup) adds implementation time. A design from scratch adds both design and development time.

### Content Readiness

If you provide all content (text, images, etc.), the project moves faster. If the freelancer needs to source or create content, expect additional cost.

### Revisions

Most freelancers include 2-3 revision rounds in their pricing. Additional rounds are typically billed hourly.

## How to Get the Best Value

1. **Have clear requirements** – The more specific you are upfront, the more accurate the quote
2. **Prepare content in advance** – Saves significant time and cost
3. **Bundle work** – Multiple pages or features in one project often cost less than separate engagements
4. **Invest in quality** – The cheapest option usually costs more in the long run through revisions, fixes, and lost performance

The right Divi freelancer is an investment in your business's online presence. A well-built Divi site pays for itself through better conversion rates, improved SEO, and a professional image that builds trust with your customers.
